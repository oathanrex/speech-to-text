Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Speech To Text ("this website", "we") handles information when you use our online speech recognition and voice typing tool.

1. Information We Handle

This website is a client-side web application. Your dictated text is processed entirely in your browser and is never uploaded to our servers. We do not operate our own backend and we do not store, sell, or share any of your notes or transcriptions.

2. Speech Recognition

Speech recognition is powered by Google Speech Recognition through the browser (requires Google Chrome). When you speak, the audio is sent by your browser directly to Google's servers over HTTPS for the sole purpose of converting your speech into text. Please review Google's Privacy Policy for details on how Google handles this audio data.

3. Text to Speech

The "Play" (text-to-speech) feature uses your browser's built-in speech synthesis. No text ever leaves your device for this feature.

4. Local Storage & Cookies

Your settings and notes may be saved in your browser's local storage so that they remain available the next time you visit. Cookies may be used by third-party services (see below) to analyse traffic and, where enabled, to serve personalised advertising.

5. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdSense)

This site may show advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve and personalise ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • To opt out of personalised advertising, visit Google Ads Settings.
  • To learn more about how Google uses information, see How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites.
  • You can also opt out of third-party cookies at aboutads.info.

6. Google Analytics

This website may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. Google Analytics collects aggregate, anonymised usage data such as pages visited and browser type. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

7. Microphone Access

We request permission to use your microphone only while you actively use dictation. The microphone is never accessed in the background. You can revoke access at any time from your browser settings.

8. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, contact us via Telegram @oathanrex or email oathanrex@gmail.com.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last modified" date.
